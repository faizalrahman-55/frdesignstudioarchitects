[01 / USING THE WEBSITE]

An introduction to the studio.

This website presents FR Design Studio’s architecture, interior design, and consultancy services. You may browse it, share links, and contact us about a potential project.

Please use the site lawfully and respectfully. Do not attempt to access restricted systems, interfere with the website, impersonate another person, or use the enquiry tools to send harmful or unlawful material.

[02 / PROJECT ENGAGEMENTS]

A conversation comes first.

Sending an enquiry starts a discussion. It does not by itself appoint the studio, reserve availability, confirm a fee, or commit either party to a project.

Before work begins, the project scope, fees, deliverables, programme, responsibilities, and any other relevant terms should be agreed separately in writing. The terms of that project agreement govern the commissioned work.

Website descriptions are general. The services appropriate for your project depend on its location, site conditions, requirements, and agreed scope.

[03 / DESIGN CONTENT]

Presentation is not construction guidance.

Photographs, drawings, models, and visualisations show design ideas or project information. A visualisation may show a proposed design rather than a completed building; refer to the context provided with each project.

Do not use website images or descriptions as construction drawings, technical specifications, planning approval, structural advice, or confirmation of compliance. A real project requires its own professional assessment, coordinated documentation, and relevant approvals.

Materials, colours, dimensions, and finishes shown online can differ from the final design or built result.

[04 / OWNERSHIP & REUSE]

Respect the work and its creators.

Website text, branding, photographs, drawings, and other creative material remain subject to the rights of FR Design Studio and their respective creators or rights holders. Display on this website does not transfer ownership or grant a licence to reproduce a design.

Please ask before republishing, adapting, or using the content commercially, unless a separate licence or applicable law permits your use. Sharing a link to the website is welcome.

Only send us files or information that you are entitled to share. If you believe material on the site infringes your rights, email frdesignstudioarchitect@gmail.com with the page address and relevant details.

[05 / EXTERNAL SERVICES]

Other services have their own terms.

Map links, email applications, and the hosting platform are operated separately from this studio website. Your use of those services is subject to their own terms and privacy practices.

The enquiry form prepares an email draft for you to review and send. It does not confirm delivery or a booking. For details about data handling, read our Security & Privacy page.

Keep the conversation clear.

We may update the website and these terms as the studio’s website or services develop. The date at the top identifies this version. Website changes do not replace a separately agreed project contract.

Nothing on this page is intended to remove rights or protections that cannot lawfully be excluded. For questions about these terms, contact frdesignstudioarchitect@gmail.com.
