[01 / YOUR ENQUIRY]

You choose when to send.

The project enquiry form prepares a draft in your email application. It does not submit your details to a website database or send a message automatically. You can review the draft before choosing to send it.

If you send the email, FR Design Studio receives the information you include, such as your name, email address, project location, selected service, and project description. This information helps us respond and discuss your project. Your email provider and the studio’s email provider also process the message under their own policies.

[02 / DOWNLOADED BRIEFS]

A copy for your own device.

The “download your brief” option creates a text file in your browser and saves it to your device. Downloading it does not send the brief to the studio. You decide whether to attach or share the file later.

The website does not save enquiry drafts in browser local storage. Your browser or email application may keep form history, drafts, or downloaded files according to your settings.

[03 / HOSTING & COOKIES]

The site and its hosting are separate.

The studio’s website code does not include advertising trackers or analytics scripts. Its images, fonts, and interaction scripts do not require external marketing services.

This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages and does not require a visitor account or sign-in. GitHub records and retains visitors’ IP addresses for security, including for visitors who are not signed in. Details about the hosting provider’s handling of information are available in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Links to maps and other external websites take you to services with their own privacy practices.

[04 / SHARING SAFELY]

Start with the essentials.

The published website uses HTTPS to protect the connection between your browser and its host. Email is a separate communication channel; sending an enquiry does not guarantee end-to-end encryption or complete confidentiality.

For a first enquiry, share a general description of your project. Please leave out passwords, payment credentials, identity documents, and confidential drawings. Contact the studio to agree on a suitable way to exchange sensitive project material.

[05 / SECURITY REPORTS]

Let us know if something looks wrong.

To report a suspected security issue with this website, email frdesignstudioarchitect@gmail.com with the subject “Website security report”. Include the affected page, a brief description, and safe steps that help us understand the issue.

Please avoid accessing other people’s data, disrupting the site, or sending sensitive information as proof. Reporting an issue does not grant permission to test the hosting platform or other third-party systems.

[06 / PRIVACY QUESTIONS]

A direct point of contact.

For questions about information you have emailed to the studio, or to request a correction or deletion, contact frdesignstudioarchitect@gmail.com. Requests can be discussed in the context of any ongoing project and applicable recordkeeping requirements.

For information handled by the hosting provider, use GitHub’s privacy information and contact channels.
